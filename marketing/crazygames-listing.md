## Title

Slide & Paint

## Short description

Roll the ball and paint every tile in this relaxing maze puzzle with 200+ levels!

## Description

Slide & Paint is a relaxing maze puzzle where one swipe sends the ball rolling, and it won't stop until it hits a wall! Every tile the ball rolls over gets painted. Plan your route to fill the whole maze with colour and clear the level.

### How to play

Swipe or use the arrow keys to roll the ball. It keeps sliding until it hits a wall, painting every tile on the way. Paint every empty tile on the board to complete the level. Take each puzzle at your own pace. There's no timer, so think ahead and find the smartest path.

### Features

- **200+ handcrafted-feel levels**, from easy warm-ups to tricky Expert mazes, plus endless levels after that
- **Every level is solvable.** You can never get stuck, and you can undo any move
- **Earn up to 3 stars** by finishing within par, the fewest moves needed
- **Daily Challenge**: a fresh puzzle every day to keep your streak going
- **Hints** show you the next moves when a maze has you stumped
- **14 collectible balls** to unlock with stars, including Beach Ball, Eight Ball, Planet, Melon and Rainbow
- **Your progress saves automatically** and syncs across devices when you're logged in to CrazyGames
- Satisfying paint effects, calm music, and a quick tutorial to get you rolling

## Controls

**Desktop**
- Arrow keys or WASD: roll the ball
- Mouse: click and drag in a direction to roll
- Z: undo the last move
- R: restart the level
- H: show a hint
- M: open the menu
- Enter / Space: next level (on the level complete screen)

**Mobile**
- Swipe up, down, left or right to roll the ball
- Tap the buttons for undo, restart and hints
